# Phan cong vai tro 4 thanh vien — SportShop

| Vai tro | Thanh vien | Trach nhiem chinh |
|---------|------------|---------------------|
| Truong nhom / PM | TV1 | Dieu phoi tien do, bao cao, quan ly Jira/backlog |
| Frontend          | TV2 | Giao dien khach hang & quan tri (thu muc `frontend/`) |
| Backend/DB        | TV3 | API, nghiep vu, co so du lieu (thu muc `backend/`, `database/`) |
| Testing/Tai lieu  | TV4 | Kiem thu tu dong (`kiemThu/`), tai lieu du an (`taiLieu/`) |

Chi tiet phan cong theo tung tuan nam trong bang "Phan cong 4 thanh vien" cua ke hoach Sprint.
