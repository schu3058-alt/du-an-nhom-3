1. Moi cong viec deu phai co issue tuong ung tren Jira (project KAN) truoc khi bat dau.
2. Tao nhanh rieng cho moi issue, khong commit truc tiep len nhanh `main`.
3. Pull Request can it nhat 1 thanh vien khac review truoc khi merge.
4. Cap nhat trang thai issue tren Jira ngay khi bat dau / hoan thanh cong viec.
5. Cuoi moi Sprint, viet bao cao ngan trong `taiLieu/baoCaoSprint/` (theo mau `sprint01.md`).
6. Moi thay doi lien quan CSDL phai cap nhat dong thoi `database/taoBang.sql` va
   `taiLieu/soDoCoSoDuLieu.md`.
